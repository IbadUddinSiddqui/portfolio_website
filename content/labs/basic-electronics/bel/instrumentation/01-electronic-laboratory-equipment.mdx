# Electronic Laboratory Equipment

## Overview

Electronic laboratory equipment forms the foundation of all practical electronics work. Proper understanding and operation of measurement instruments are essential for circuit testing, troubleshooting, and verification. This laboratory session introduces the standard electronic test and measurement equipment found in electronics laboratories.

## Objective

To practice using standard electronic laboratory equipment including the digital multimeter (DMM), dual-channel oscilloscope, and DC power supply.

## Domain

Instrumentation — Electronic Test and Measurement Equipment.

## CLO and Taxonomy Level

- **CLO-3**: Apply standard laboratory equipment for electronic circuit measurement and testing.
- **Taxonomy Level**: P3 — Supervised Practice (Practical application of measurement techniques under guidance).

## Components and Equipment

- **Instruments**: Digital Multimeter (DMM), Dual-channel oscilloscope, DC power supply, Function generator
- **Accessories**: Oscilloscope probes (10×/1×), Multimeter test leads, BNC-to-alligator cables, Banana plug cables

## Required Skills

- Basic understanding of voltage, current, and resistance
- Safety awareness when working with electrical circuits
- Careful handling of precision instruments

## Theory and Engineering Concepts

- **Digital Multimeter (DMM)**: Measures voltage (DC/AC), current (DC/AC), and resistance. Proper range selection prevents damage and ensures accuracy. The DMM has a high input impedance (>10MΩ) for voltage measurement to minimize circuit loading.
- **Oscilloscope**: Displays voltage signals over time, allowing visualization of waveforms, measurement of amplitude, frequency, period, and phase relationships. Key controls include Volts/Div, Time/Div, trigger level, and coupling (AC/DC/GND).
- **DC Power Supply**: Provides regulated DC voltage and current for powering circuits. Can operate in constant voltage (CV) or constant current (CC) mode.
- **Function Generator**: Produces various waveforms (sine, square, triangle) at specified frequencies and amplitudes for testing circuits.

## Procedure / Implementation

### Step 1: Digital Multimeter Familiarization

1. Identify the main sections of the DMM:
   - Display (LCD/LED readout)
   - Function selector switch (V, A, Ω, etc.)
   - Input jacks (COM, V/Ω, A, mA)
2. Measure a known DC voltage:
   - Set the function selector to DC voltage (V⎓)
   - Connect the red probe to V/Ω jack and black probe to COM jack
   - Connect probes across the DC power supply output set to 5V
   - Read and record the displayed voltage
3. Measure resistance:
   - Set the function selector to resistance (Ω)
   - Touch probes across a 1kΩ resistor
   - Confirm reading of approximately 1000Ω

### Step 2: Oscilloscope Familiarization

1. Identify main controls:
   - Vertical controls: Position, Volts/Div, coupling (AC/DC/GND), channel enable
   - Horizontal controls: Position, Time/Div, horizontal mode
   - Trigger controls: Source, level, slope, mode (Auto/Normal/Single)
2. Connect the function generator output to Channel 1 (CH1)
3. Set the function generator to output a 1kHz sine wave at 2Vpp
4. Adjust oscilloscope controls:
   - Set Volts/Div to 0.5V/Div
   - Set Time/Div to 0.5ms/Div
   - Adjust trigger level for a stable display
5. Measure the signal amplitude and frequency using on-screen cursors or graticule

### Step 3: DC Power Supply Operation

1. Identify controls:
   - Voltage adjustment knob
   - Current limit adjustment knob
   - Output enable/disable switch
   - Display mode (V/I)
2. Set the power supply to output 12V:
   - Turn the voltage adjustment while monitoring the display
   - Enable the output
   - Verify the voltage using the DMM

## What Was Done

Familiarized with the operation of a digital multimeter (DMM), dual-channel oscilloscope, and DC power supply. Practiced voltage, resistance, and waveform measurements. Configured the oscilloscope to display a 1kHz sine wave and measured its amplitude and frequency.

## What Was Learnt

- Proper measurement techniques, including setting the DMM to appropriate ranges
- Using the oscilloscope to visualize waveforms and measure frequency characteristics
- Safe operation of DC power supplies for circuit powering
- The importance of instrument calibration and probe compensation
- Correct probe selection (1× vs 10×) for different measurement scenarios
