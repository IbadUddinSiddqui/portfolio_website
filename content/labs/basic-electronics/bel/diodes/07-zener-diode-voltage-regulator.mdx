# Zener Diode Voltage Regulator

## Overview

Voltage regulation is essential in electronic systems to maintain a stable DC supply voltage despite variations in input voltage or load current. The Zener diode, operating in reverse breakdown mode, provides a simple and effective voltage regulation solution for low-power applications.

## Objective

To practice utilizing a Zener diode as a functional voltage regulator.

## Domain

Power Electronics — Voltage Regulation and Power Supply Design.

## CLO and Taxonomy Level

- **CLO-3**: Design and analyze Zener diode voltage regulator circuits.
- **Taxonomy Level**: P3 — Supervised Practice (Practical application of voltage regulator under guidance).

## Components and Equipment

- **Semiconductors**: 5.1V Zener diode (1N4733A or equivalent)
- **Passive Components**: 220Ω series resistor, 1kΩ load resistor
- **Equipment**: Variable DC power supply, DMM, Oscilloscope

## Required Skills

- Diode forward and reverse bias behavior
- Understanding of series and parallel circuits
- Ohm's law and power calculations

## Theory and Engineering Concepts

- **Zener Breakdown**: When a Zener diode is reverse-biased beyond its Zener voltage (Vz), it conducts current while maintaining a nearly constant voltage across its terminals.
- **Regulation Mechanism**: The series resistor drops the excess voltage (Vin - Vz). When Vin increases, the Zener current increases but Vz remains constant; when the load current changes, the Zener current adjusts to maintain constant output.
- **Line Regulation**: The ability to maintain constant output voltage despite changes in input voltage. Measured in mV/V or percentage.
- **Load Regulation**: The ability to maintain constant output voltage despite changes in load current.
- **Series Resistor Selection**: Rs = (Vin(min) - Vz) / (Iz(min) + Iload(max))

## Circuit Configuration

The Zener regulator consists of:
- Series resistor (Rs) from the unregulated input to the Zener cathode
- Zener diode with cathode to Rs/anode to ground
- Load resistor in parallel with the Zener diode
- Output voltage taken across the Zener diode

## Procedure / Implementation

### Step 1: Circuit Construction

1. Connect the series resistor (220Ω) to the positive output of the DC power supply
2. Connect the Zener diode cathode (banded end) to the series resistor
3. Connect the Zener diode anode to ground
4. Connect the load resistor (1kΩ) in parallel with the Zener diode
5. Verify the circuit: Input → Rs → Zener cathode, Zener anode → GND, Output across Zener

### Step 2: Line Regulation Test

1. Set the power supply to 8V
2. Measure the output voltage across the Zener diode (should be ≈5.1V)
3. Increase the input voltage in 1V steps from 8V to 15V
4. Record the output voltage at each step
5. Calculate line regulation: ΔVout / ΔVin (mV/V)

### Step 3: Load Regulation Test

1. Set the input voltage to a fixed value (e.g., 12V)
2. Measure the output voltage with no load (Iload = 0)
3. Connect different load resistors and measure the output voltage for each
4. Record output voltage vs. load current

## What Was Done

Designed a circuit using a 5.1V Zener diode (1N4733A) in reverse breakdown mode to maintain a constant output voltage across a load. Tested line regulation (input variation) and load regulation (load variation).

## What Was Learnt

- Zener diodes are ideal for voltage regulation due to their ability to maintain a steady voltage despite input fluctuations or load variations
- The series resistor is critical for limiting current through the Zener diode
- Line regulation shows how well the regulator rejects input voltage changes
- Load regulation shows how well the regulator maintains voltage with varying load current
- Zener regulators are best suited for low-power applications due to power dissipation limits
